The Story

The lighthouse at the edge of the world has gone dark. Lume, a small forgotten robot, wakes up with a single purpose: reach the top and light it again.

Along the way lie the remains of the Wardens — the guardian robots who once kept the light alive. Each one holds a fragment of memory. Piece them together, and you'll learn why the lighthouse fell silent.
